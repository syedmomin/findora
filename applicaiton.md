# Brand Vision

Findora is not just an OCR scanner.

It is a premium Android productivity tool that allows users to instantly find information hidden inside screenshots, PDFs, photos, receipts, IDs, and documents using fast on-device OCR and intelligent search.

The experience should feel as polished as apps like:

- Linear
- Arc Browser
- Notion
- Dropbox
- Airbnb
- Google Files

---

# Design Principles

### Minimal

No unnecessary UI.

---

### Fast

---

### Premium

---

### Privacy First

# Logo Guidelines

The logo should represent **Discovery**, **Organization**, and **Precision**.

### Symbol

A custom geometric **F**.

Not a normal font.

The **F** should be merged into a unique geometric mark.

Characteristics:

- Rounded geometry
- Balanced spacing
- Optical alignment
- Premium silhouette
- Recognizable at 48×48 pixels

No:

- Magnifying glass
- Folder icon
- Document icon
- OCR letters
- Camera icon
- Generic search icon

The logo should be memorable enough to stand alone without text.
